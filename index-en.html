Process, legal, tax

The trust section.

A public auction under 127-FZ, a clean title mechanism and a tax treatment that matters to a buyer without input VAT deduction.

01

Legal cleanliness guaranteed

Under Federal Law No. 127-FZ on insolvency, the seller’s tax debts, pledges, attachments and encumbrances are extinguished on purchase and the price is protected from challenge.

02

Purchase procedure

Public auction, open bidding, at the threshold price. The process is transparent and the price is the auction threshold.

03

Application deadline

Applications are accepted until 25 November 2026. Missing the date is the only way to lose the asset.

Tax note — precisely

Sale of a bankrupt debtor’s property is not subject to VAT (Clause 15, Sub-clause 2, Article 146 of the Russian Tax Code). For a buyer with no right to deduct input VAT this is worth 16.7% of the price (RUB 368,376,909 vs. RUB 442,052,291 with VAT).

Free-port (SPV) and Advanced Development Territory (TOR) preferences — zero property tax for 5 years, zero land tax for 5 years, reduced social contributions from 30% to 7.6% — require registration of the asset under a dedicated project, resident status, and a tax opinion. They are a regime for a resident, not an automatic entitlement of the buyer.

Source: investment memorandum; Russian Tax Code, Article 146, Clause 15, Sub-clause 2.
